Limits, Continuity & L'Hôpital's Rule

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = 1$$
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos(x)}{x} = 0$$
$$\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^x = e = \lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{1/x}$$
$$\text{If } \lim_{x \to c} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{0}{0} \text{ or } \frac{\pm\infty}{\pm\infty}, \text{ then } \lim_{x \to c} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to c} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} \quad \text{(L'Hôpital's Rule)}$$

Trigonometric Powers & Product Integrals

$$\int \sin^2(x) \, dx = \frac{x}{2} - \frac{\sin(2x)}{4} + C$$
$$\int \cos^2(x) \, dx = \frac{x}{2} + \frac{\sin(2x)}{4} + C$$
$$\int \tan^2(x) \, dx = \tan(x) - x + C$$
$$\int \sec^2(x) \, dx = \tan(x) + C$$
$$\int \csc^2(x) \, dx = -\cot(x) + C$$
$$\int \cot^2(x) \, dx = -\cot(x) - x + C$$
$$\int \sin^3(x) \, dx = -\frac{3\cos(x)}{4} + \frac{\cos(3x)}{12} + C$$
$$\int \cos^3(x) \, dx = \frac{3\sin(x)}{4} + \frac{\sin(3x)}{12} + C$$
$$\int \sec^3(x) \, dx = \frac{1}{2}\sec(x)\tan(x) + \frac{1}{2}\ln\lvert\sec(x)+\tan(x)\rvert + C$$
$$\int \csc^3(x) \, dx = -\frac{1}{2}\csc(x)\cot(x) - \frac{1}{2}\ln\lvert\csc(x)+\cot(x)\rvert + C$$
$$\int \sec(x)\tan(x) \, dx = \sec(x) + C$$
$$\int \csc(x)\cot(x) \, dx = -\csc(x) + C$$

General Reduction Formulas ($n \ge 2$)

$$\int \sin^n(x) \, dx = -\frac{\sin^{n-1}(x)\cos(x)}{n} + \frac{n-1}{n}\int \sin^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$
$$\int \cos^n(x) \, dx = \frac{\cos^{n-1}(x)\sin(x)}{n} + \frac{n-1}{n}\int \cos^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$
$$\int \tan^n(x) \, dx = \frac{\tan^{n-1}(x)}{n-1} - \int \tan^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$
$$\int \sec^n(x) \, dx = \frac{\sec^{n-2}(x)\tan(x)}{n-1} + \frac{n-2}{n-1}\int \sec^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$
$$\int \csc^n(x) \, dx = -\frac{\csc^{n-2}(x)\cot(x)}{n-1} + \frac{n-2}{n-1}\int \csc^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$
$$\int \cot^n(x) \, dx = -\frac{\cot^{n-1}(x)}{n-1} - \int \cot^{n-2}(x) \, dx$$

Mixed Frequencies & Exponential-Trig Products

$$\int \sin(ax)\cos(bx) \, dx = -\frac{\cos((a-b)x)}{2(a-b)} - \frac{\cos((a+b)x)}{2(a+b)} + C$$
$$\int \sin(ax)\sin(bx) \, dx = \frac{\sin((a-b)x)}{2(a-b)} - \frac{\sin((a+b)x)}{2(a+b)} + C$$
$$\int \cos(ax)\cos(bx) \, dx = \frac{\sin((a-b)x)}{2(a-b)} + \frac{\sin((a+b)x)}{2(a+b)} + C$$
$$\int e^{ax} \sin(bx) \, dx = \frac{e^{ax}(a\sin(bx) - b\cos(bx))}{a^2 + b^2} + C$$
$$\int e^{ax} \cos(bx) \, dx = \frac{e^{ax}(a\cos(bx) + b\sin(bx))}{a^2 + b^2} + C$$
$$\int x \sin(ax) \, dx = \frac{\sin(ax)}{a^2} - \frac{x\cos(ax)}{a} + C$$

Substitutions & Euler Complex Identities

$$\text{Let } t = \tan\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) \implies dx = \frac{2}{1+t^2} dt \quad \text{(Weierstrass Substitution)}$$
$$\sin(x) = \frac{2t}{1+t^2}, \quad \cos(x) = \frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}, \quad \tan(x) = \frac{2t}{1-t^2}$$
$$e^{ix} = \cos(x) + i\sin(x) \quad \text{(Euler's Identity)}$$
$$\sin(x) = \frac{e^{ix} - e^{-ix}}{2i}, \quad \cos(x) = \frac{e^{ix} + e^{-ix}}{2}$$
$$\sinh(x) = \frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2}, \quad \cosh(x) = \frac{e^x + e^{-x}}{2}$$
$$\int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^n(x) dx = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \cos^n(x) dx \quad \text{(Wallis' Integrals)}$$

Sequences, Series & Taylor Expansions

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n = \frac{a}{1-r} \quad (\lvert r\rvert < 1) \quad \text{(Geometric Series)}$$
$$e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^3}{3!} + \dots$$
$$\sin(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!} = x - \frac{x^3}{3!} + \frac{x^5}{5!} - \dots$$
$$\cos(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n x^{2n}}{(2n)!} = 1 - \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^4}{4!} - \dots$$
$$\frac{1}{1-x} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x^n = 1 + x + x^2 + x^3 + \dots \quad (\lvert x\rvert < 1)$$

Multivariable & Vector Calculus Theorems

$$\nabla f = \left\langle \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \right\rangle \quad \text{(Gradient)}$$
$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z} \quad \text{(Divergence)}$$
$$\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \left(\frac{\partial R}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial Q}{\partial z}\right)\mathbf{i} + \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial R}{\partial x}\right)\mathbf{j} + \left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)\mathbf{k} \quad \text{(Curl)}$$
$$\oint_C (L \, dx + M \, dy) = \iint_D \left(\frac{\partial M}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial L}{\partial y}\right) dA \quad \text{(Green's Theorem)}$$
$$\iint_S \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_V (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}) \, dV \quad \text{(Divergence / Gauss's Theorem)}$$
$$\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot d\mathbf{S} \quad \text{(Stokes' Theorem)}$$
